Loopy

Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Loopy is an app for making progress on personal and team projects. This policy explains what data it uses, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. The person responsible is Luis Agustín Álvarez Ibaceta. For any privacy questions, write to loopy.support.app@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Loopy has no advertising, does not track you, and does not use third-party analytics services.
  • Your projects, check-ins, and notes are stored on this iPhone and in your iCloud. We cannot read them.
  • What you share with a team is visible to the people on that team.
  • Your social profile (name search, streak, and XP) is off until you enable it.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Settings › Delete my account.

What data Loopy uses and why

Your account

Loopy uses your iCloud account to sync your data. We do not ask for an email address or password. To identify you, the app creates an internal Loopy identifier. We do not know your Apple ID.

Your name

We only ask you for a name when another person is going to see it: when you create or join a team, or add a friend. That name and your Loopy identifier are stored in Loopy's public database in iCloud so that your friends and teams can recognize you. Other people can read that record if they know your identifier, but they cannot find you by searching for your name unless you enable your social profile. We, as the people responsible for the app, can also see that record.

Social profile (optional)

If you enable Settings › Privacy › Social profile, anyone who uses Loopy can find you by name and see your streak and XP. If you turn it off, you stop appearing in search and that data is removed from the public record.

Projects, check-ins, journal, and notes

These are stored on this iPhone and in your private iCloud database. Apple stores them for you, and we do not have access to them. Notes marked as private are never shared.

Team projects

When you invite someone or join a team, the project's content is shared with its members through iCloud: name and goal, milestones, check-ins, shared journal entries, and the project's source document or text. Each member sees that content in their own app. If someone opens an invitation link, they join the team. That is why you should share links only with the people you want.

Documents

If you attach a PDF or paste text to describe a project, Loopy reads it on your iPhone. The extracted text is stored in the project and is visible to its team members. The original file is not uploaded.

Apple Intelligence

Suggestions and reflection questions are generated with Apple's models that run on the device. That text is not sent to us or to third parties. Without Apple Intelligence, you can use the app manually.

Dictation

If you use “Dictate,” Loopy asks for permission to use your microphone and speech recognition. The audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition service, which may process it on the device or on Apple's servers depending on your iPhone, in accordance with Apple's privacy policy. Loopy does not store the audio, only the text you confirm.

Purchases

Payments are processed by the App Store. We do not receive your card details. Loopy verifies purchases with StoreKit. To sync group access, it stores the organizer, transaction identifier, its revision, and the effective expiration or grace-period date with the project. That data is shared with project members through iCloud; it does not include payment details or provide access to the organizer's App Store account. An account identifier derived from your iCloud identity links the subscription to the organizer.

Reports

If you report a person or content, we store the report in Loopy's public database in iCloud. The report includes your identifier, the reported person's identifier, the reason, and a copy of the reported text. It can be read only by you and by us. We use it to review the case and take action, and we delete it 12 months after closing it.

Notifications

If you enable them, Loopy reminds you about your check-ins and notifies you of friend requests. Notifications are delivered by Apple (iCloud and Apple's notification service).

Who data is shared with

We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties. Your data is stored by Apple (iCloud) as a provider for the app. Apart from that, it is only visible to the people you choose to share with: your team and, if you enable your social profile, people who use Loopy.

How long we keep data

  • While you have your account, your data stays in your iCloud.
  • Deleting your account deletes your public profile, friend requests, check-ins, journal entries, notes, source document, and the names of milestones you created in other people's projects.
  • If you owned a team project, we give another person on the team up to 14 days to take ownership of it. After that period, the copy is deleted even if no one has taken it. If you delete Loopy sooner, you can also delete it yourself in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage storage › Loopy.
  • What other people wrote in a team belongs to them and is not deleted with your account.

Your rights and how to withdraw your consent

  • Social profile: turn it off in Settings › Privacy.
  • Microphone, speech, and notifications: revoke their permissions in iPhone Settings › Loopy.
  • Leave a team: from the project, choose Leave project.
  • Delete everything: go to Settings › Delete my account.
  • Access, correction, or any other request: write to loopy.support.app@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Minors

Loopy is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will also notify you in the app.
