Terms of use

Version 1 · Effective October 15, 2026

Loopy is an app for making progress on personal and team projects. These terms explain what you can and cannot do in Loopy. The person responsible is Luis Agustín Álvarez Ibaceta. For any questions, write to loopy.support.app@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Using Loopy just for yourself does not require you to accept anything else.
  • Before you share anything with other people (create or join a team, send invitations, add friends, or enable your social profile), Loopy asks you to accept these terms.
  • Zero tolerance: offensive content and abusive people are not allowed.
  • You can report and block anyone. We review every report in less than 24 hours.

Accepting these terms

When you tap “I accept” in the app, you accept these terms and the privacy policy. If you do not accept them, you can keep using Loopy for your personal projects, but you cannot share with other people.

What you share

What you write in a team project (check-ins, journal, milestones, the project brief) and your name are visible to the people on that team. If you enable your social profile, anyone who uses Loopy can find you by name. What you write belongs to you, and you are responsible for it. Only share what you have the right to share.

Zero tolerance for offensive content and abusive people

In Loopy, you may not publish any of the following in any name, text, or document that other people can see:

  • hate speech or discrimination based on origin, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other status;
  • harassment, intimidation, humiliation, or threats against anyone;
  • sexually explicit content, or any sexual content involving minors;
  • incitement to violence, self-harm, or suicide;
  • another person's personal information without their permission;
  • impersonation, spam, or deception;
  • any content that is illegal or infringes other people's rights.

How we care for the community

  • Filter: Loopy uses your iPhone to check the text that other people will see and prevents you from saving insults, expressions of hate, or threats.
  • Report: on a person's profile, tap ⋯ › Report. For a journal entry, milestone, or teammate's project brief, press and hold or open its menu and choose Report. Reported content is hidden from you immediately.
  • Block: on the person's profile, tap ⋯ › Block. They will not be able to find or invite you, and you will not see what they write in shared projects.
  • Review: we review every report in less than 24 hours. If someone does not follow these terms, we may remove their public profile, suspend their profile so that no one can find them or receive their requests, and ask the team coordinator to remove them from the project. In the event of threats or a risk to a minor, we act immediately and notify the authorities when required by law.

Teams

The person who coordinates a project decides who joins and can remove anyone from the team. An invitation link lets anyone who opens it join, so share it only with the people you want.

Loopy Groups

Individual projects are free. Loopy Groups is a monthly subscription with automatic renewal for the organizer: it enables collaboration on the group projects they own, with invitations, shared direction and milestones, check-ins, and the team's journal, subject to the team capacity shown in the app. Invited people participate for free. The subscription covers these collaboration tools and their regular improvements; it does not promise to include all future paid offerings.

The App Store shows the localized price and period before confirmation and processes charges. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel through your Apple account's subscriptions. Canceling preserves access until the end of the paid period. If Apple confirms a grace period, access continues until that date. When access expires or is revoked, the group's history remains readable and ordinary collaboration is paused; renewing reactivates it in the same projects. If project ownership changes, the new person's access applies. You can keep using individual projects and account and safety actions.

In Settings › Loopy Groups, you can restore access and manage your subscription. Purchases are governed by Apple's terms, including the Licensed Application End User License Agreement (Apple's standard EULA). Refunds must be requested from Apple.

Your account

You can stop using Loopy whenever you want and delete your account in Settings › Delete my account. The privacy policy explains what is deleted.

Changes

If we change these terms, we will update the version and date above. If the change is significant, Loopy will ask you to accept them again before sharing.
