Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Go to Settings › Loopy Groups › Restore purchases, using the Apple account used for the purchase and the organizer's iCloud account. Loopy Groups enables collaboration on your group projects; the people you invite participate for free.

How do I cancel Loopy Groups?

Go to Settings › Loopy Groups › Manage subscription. Renewal is monthly and automatic. Canceling keeps your access until the effective date through which you have paid; a verified grace period also preserves access. If it expires, you can read the team's history and renew to resume activity in the same projects. Individual projects and account and safety actions remain available.

The group appears to be paused

Access depends on the subscription of the person who owns the project. If you are a member, you do not need to make a purchase: let the organizer know. If verification or iCloud is unavailable, Loopy uses the last verified access only until its known end date. Connect and restore purchases to update it. Transferring the project applies the new person's subscription.

How do I report someone or offensive content?

On the person's profile, tap ⋯ and then Report. For a journal entry, tap its day on the project board, press and hold the entry, and choose Report. For a milestone, open it in Project settings › Milestones, tap ⋯, and choose Report. The content is hidden from you immediately, and we review it in less than 24 hours. You can also block the person: they will no longer see you, and you will not see what they write in shared projects.

How do I remove someone from my team?

If you coordinate the project, open the team (the avatars at the top of the project, or Project settings › Invite by link), press and hold the person's name, and choose Remove from team. They will no longer see the project or the team's journal, and they cannot rejoin even if they have the link. What they have already written stays in the project. You can also report them from the same menu.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings › Delete my account. Our privacy policy explains what is deleted and what happens to your team projects.

Loopy is not syncing

Check that you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is enabled for Loopy in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud.
